SQL parser supporting DDL (CREATE TABLE, DROP TABLE, ALTER TABLE), DML (INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE), and DQL (SELECT with WHERE, COUNT(*) aggregation, and vector distance expressions).

Parses SQL text into an AST of Statement variants consumed by the executor.
